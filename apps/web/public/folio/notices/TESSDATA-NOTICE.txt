Tesseract traineddata for English and Indonesian.

Source: @tesseract.js-data/eng 1.0.0 and @tesseract.js-data/ind 1.0.0,
which carry the 4.0.0_best_int traineddata from the Tesseract project.
License: Apache-2.0. The license text is TESSERACT-APACHE-2.0.txt in this
folder.

Folio copies eng.traineddata.gz and ind.traineddata.gz into /folio/ocr/ at
build time. It does not send a page image or OCR text to any origin.
