# Tool Catalog (/)

## Scope

- Surface: the home page at `/`, the Tool Catalog. Stage 3b of the Ritools redesign.
- Mode: Persuade. The visitor decides to open a Tool; no task is completed on this page.
- Tools today: one (Squeeze). The page must look complete with one and hold ten.
- Shell unchanged. A Tool switcher is agreed as a later shell change: a plain "Tools" mark beside the wordmark opens a menu of every Tool, with the current one marked. Not built here.
- Copy approved in en-US and id-ID. The h1 stays the plain text "Tools" / "Alat" so the home and SSR e2e tests keep their contract.
- SSR stays on. Head title stays `Tools | Ritools`, with the current factual description.

## Audience and job

A first-time visitor, often arriving from a Tool found in search. The page must say what Ritools is, prove the local-only claim, and send the visitor into a Tool. The second job is discovery for the Tools that follow.

## Outcome and proof

- Primary action: open Squeeze, from a visible primary mark in the Tool proof.
- Success: one viewport tells what Ritools is, that the files stay on the device, and that Squeeze exists today.
- Proof: the real Squeeze facts (input formats, output formats, three size modes) and the local-only claim. No invented numbers, users, benchmarks, or testimonials.

## States and ranges

- Tool counts: 1 (today), 10 (later). Proof rows repeat; the sidebar note and the stamp stay fixed.
- Both color modes, 390 px to 1600 px, keyboard alone. No loading state: the page is static and server-rendered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalog is a printer's proof sheet. Every Tool is a numbered proof on one shared rule grid, and the local-only claim prints as the sheet's stamp. It refuses the card grid with one lonely card and a "coming soon" gap.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground under the red navbar. 2px ink rules close one sheet: a red stamp plate, a proof column, and a ruled note sidebar. Ransom lettering only on Tool names. The one numerals face in fixed columns. Red fills, ink rules, white stock, zero radius, and no shadow.

STORY: The visitor reads three things in one viewport: Ritools is a set of small tools, the files never leave the device, and Squeeze is the Tool to open. The proof rows make the next Tools a continuation of the same sheet, not a gap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The page-title course holds the plain h1 "Tools" in poster type, the one-line deck, and the count `01 tool` at the left, beside a red stamp plate that reads LOCAL ONLY, the three promises, and "The only cap is your device." A 3px rule closes the course. Below, the sheet: Squeeze prints as proof `01` in a wide cell with its ransom name, promise, three fact rows, and the primary mark `Open Squeeze`; a ruled note cell sits beside it and states the browser and tab facts. A closing rule carries "More tools are on the way."

FORM: Proof Sheet, dealt lead (index 3 of 7 grounded candidates), seed key 331669c6, code-led, no comp. Signature interaction: the stamp presses as the name assembles. The plate settles from 1.12 scale with a 2px drop, the count numeral lands, and the top rule prints from the left. The sequence runs once. Reduced motion holds the composed sheet.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
